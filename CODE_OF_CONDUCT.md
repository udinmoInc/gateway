# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
version 2.1.

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in
our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may
be reported to the Udinmo Inc maintainers via the repository's issue or
security channels. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated
promptly and fairly.

Project maintainers who fail to follow this Code of Conduct may face
temporary or permanent consequences as determined by the project
maintainer (Vijay Singh / Udinmo Inc).
